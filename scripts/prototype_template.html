How it works. Each candidate site is scored by how much it raises the probability of survival for nearby cardiac arrests if a bystander walks to fetch its AED, compared with waiting for the ambulance that actually arrived (Valenzuela et al. 1997 survival model, real walking routes). Sites are added one at a time by a greedy algorithm, which matched the exact integer-program optimum on this data; the indoor strategy only counts a site if it was open at the time of the 911 call. Gains are potential survivors: they assume a shockable rhythm and a willing bystander, so real-world numbers are smaller.
